# Retail Sales Analytics – Initial Findings

## Category Performance

* Technology generated the highest sales ($827,456).
* Furniture generated $728,659.
* Office Supplies generated $705,422.

## Regional Performance

* West region generated the highest sales ($710,220).
* East region followed with $669,519.
* South region generated the lowest sales ($389,151).

## State Performance

Top 3 states by sales:

1. California ($446,306)
2. New York ($306,361)
3. Texas ($168,573)

## Recommendations

* Continue investing in Technology products, as they are the strongest revenue driver.
* Expand successful strategies from the West region into weaker regions.
* Investigate opportunities to increase sales in the South region.
* Focus customer acquisition efforts in high-performing states while identifying growth opportunities in mid-performing markets.

# Retail Sales Analytics - Business Insights

## Category Analysis

Technology generated the highest sales, followed by Furniture and Office Supplies. Technology products should remain a primary focus for marketing and inventory planning.

## Regional Analysis

The West region generated the highest sales, followed by the East region. The South region recorded the lowest sales and may require targeted marketing initiatives to improve performance.

## State Analysis

California was the highest-performing state, followed by New York and Texas. These states represent major revenue centers and should receive continued strategic attention.

## Customer Segment Analysis

Consumer customers generated the highest sales, significantly outperforming Corporate and Home Office customers. Future customer acquisition campaigns should prioritize the Consumer segment while retaining high-value Corporate customers.

## Product Analysis

Phones and Chairs were the top-performing sub-categories. Storage, Tables, and Binders also contributed substantial revenue. Maintaining stock availability and creating product bundles around these categories could increase overall sales.

## Overall Recommendation

The business should focus on strengthening Technology product offerings, expanding market share in high-performing regions, and leveraging Consumer-focused marketing strategies. Product bundling and targeted promotions in underperforming regions may further increase revenue.
## Monthly Sales Trend Analysis

The monthly sales trend indicates an overall growth in revenue from 2015 to 2018. Sales fluctuate throughout the period, with several pronounced peaks occurring near the end of each year. The highest monthly sales were recorded in late 2018, reaching approximately 118,000.

The pattern suggests the presence of seasonal demand, where sales increase during specific periods of the year. These peaks may be associated with holiday shopping seasons, promotional campaigns, or increased business spending toward year-end.

### Key Findings

* Overall sales increased over the four-year period.
* Multiple seasonal peaks are visible across years.
* The strongest sales performance occurred in late 2018.
* Monthly sales exhibit volatility but maintain an upward long-term trend.

### Business Recommendations

* Increase inventory levels before expected peak sales periods.
* Allocate additional marketing resources during high-demand seasons.
* Investigate factors contributing to the strong performance in late 2018 and replicate successful strategies.
* Use historical sales patterns to improve forecasting and operational planning.
